## What even is OOP? And why I have the time to think about this in my last month of college.

I'm sitting on a stopped Amtrak train, heading down to San Diego for my brothers graduation. Apparently someone set the tracks on fire. Anywho, I'm here about OOP. 

## I think OOP is my greatest enemy 
Maybe it's becuase I despise Java. But that's not the real truth behind all of this. It's a chicken and the egg typa situatione. You see, I took  an OOP class in second year of college, and that class was in Java. I despised that class. You see the problem here?

Now, in defense of that class, it was somewhat useful. And the professor was nice. So what more can you ask for at the end of the day. But something was missing from that whole expiernce. Nothing seemed to really click in my mind, like there was a missing join between the way that I thought about progammng and the way OOP told me to think about programming. 

On top of these thoughts floating around with my brain was my recent fascination with the concept of information silos, and I've started to relate these two topics. Not in a technical sense, mind you, but in a design and approach sense.
